ECEN403 — Subsystem 1: Baseline & Monitoring

Five benign pod datasets processed into 5-second windows.
Eight statistical features per pod, 40 total.
40/40 feature statistics independently reproduced.
Median + scaled MAD used primarily, with standard deviation fallback when MAD=0.
38 usable scales; two zero-variance features unscorable.
Prometheus successfully collected all five sources.
Frozen median metrics showed zero changes during a 5-minute observation.

LIMITATIONS
Only 66 processed windows total, including 21 held-out windows.
13/21 held-out benign windows exceeded at least one 3-scale feature threshold.
Robot-controller traffic distribution changed substantially between training and held-out windows.
A single TCP flow dominated recorded byte volume during later benign windows.
65,555-byte frame records may reflect capture or offloading characteristics; cause unverified.
No labeled attack data available; attack-detection performance has not been established.

CONCLUSION
The baseline pipeline is mathematically reproducible and operational in Prometheus. Its representativeness of all normal workload conditions remains unvalidated. Longer independent benign captures and capture-method clarification are needed.
